# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Training

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete aiming to support marathon training with high-endurance carbohydrate loading:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3200 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (130g), Fat (85g), Carbs (420g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (7:00 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (150g), honey (30g), and whole milk (250g). Side of whole wheat toast (60g) with almond butter (20g).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 15g, Carbs: 95g

* **Mid-Morning Snack (10:00 AM):** Energy bar (65g) and orange juice (250ml).
    * Protein: 8g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 65g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Grilled chicken breast (150g), brown rice (120g dry weight), roasted sweet potato (200g), and steamed broccoli (150g). Olive oil drizzle (15ml).
    * Protein: 45g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Pre-Workout Snack (3:30 PM):** Rice cakes (50g) with peanut butter (25g) and strawberries (100g).
    * Protein: 10g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 50g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Baked salmon (140g), pasta (100g dry weight), marinara sauce (150g), and mixed green salad (200g) with balsamic vinaigrette (20ml).
    * Protein: 35g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 85g

* **Evening Snack (9:00 PM):** Greek yogurt (200g) with granola (40g) and blueberries (80g).
    * Protein: 14g, Fat: 20g, Carbs: 30g

This meal plan emphasizes complex carbohydrates and whole grains to fuel endurance training, includes adequate protein for muscle recovery, and provides sustained energy throughout the day to support marathon preparation.
